# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads define and govern quality and acceptance practices across the project. They ensure the team is shipping features that meet user expectations and release standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage goals, and release quality gates
- Coordinate test planning for unit, integration, and end-to-end validation
- Review acceptance criteria with Product Managers and Developers
- Identify quality risks and escalate issues that may block release
- Support triage and regression analysis during feature delivery

### Goals
- Reduce escaped defects and regression risk
- Improve confidence in releases and production readiness
- Align testing effort with product and engineering priorities

### Typical Communication
- Daily or sprint-level quality checks with the delivery team
- Acceptance review sessions with Product Managers and stakeholders
- Release readiness updates with PMs and engineering leads

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers to define testable acceptance criteria and validate bug fixes
- Partners with Product Managers to confirm customer-facing requirements are correctly tested
- Reports risks and release blockers to Project Managers for escalation and planning

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Agile Coaches support team health, workflow consistency, and continuous improvement. They help the team remove obstacles and improve delivery effectiveness without owning product direction.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate standups, sprint planning, demos, and retrospectives
- Help the team identify blockers, dependencies, and workflow inefficiencies
- Coach the team on agile practices and effective collaboration
- Support improvement of delivery rituals and process transparency
- Coordinate with Project Managers to maintain predictable delivery cadence

### Goals
- Improve team flow and predictability
- Reduce friction in collaboration and decision-making
- Strengthen a learning-oriented, high-performing team culture

### Typical Communication
- Team ceremonies and coaching sessions
- Retrospective follow-ups and backlog refinement support
- Weekly cross-functional collaboration with PMs and delivery leaders

### Interaction with existing roles
- Supports Developers by keeping sprint execution focused and reducing meeting churn
- Works with Product Managers to improve backlog readiness and decision-making quality
- Partners with Project Managers to align delivery cadence with program goals and risks

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects shape the technical direction of the project and help ensure the solution is feasible, scalable, and maintainable. They translate business needs into technical decisions and guardrails.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction, architectural patterns, and design standards
- Review major design decisions and technical trade-offs with the delivery team
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and integration concerns early
- Support Developers in problem solving and implementation quality
- Align technical choices with product goals, compliance needs, and operational constraints

### Goals
- Deliver a maintainable and scalable technical solution
- Reduce avoidable rework and architecture drift
- Improve confidence in execution across delivery teams

### Typical Communication
- Architecture discussions and design reviews
- Technical risk assessments and dependency planning
- Cross-team coordination for platform, integration, and security concerns

### Interaction with existing roles
- Guides Developers on implementation choices and technical constraints
- Provides Product Managers with trade-off insight to support roadmap and prioritization decisions
- Collaborates with Project Managers on sequencing, dependencies, and delivery risk assessment

---

## Sponsor / Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors and Stakeholders represent the business or organizational goals behind the work. They provide sponsorship, direction, and decision-making support to keep the work aligned with strategic outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define strategic priorities, funding, and business objectives
- Approve high-level scope, milestones, and key trade-offs
- Provide decision support for escalations and policy conflicts
- Review outcomes against business value and expected benefits
- Sponsor cross-functional alignment across departments or partner teams

### Goals
- Ensure projects remain aligned to business needs and strategic priorities
- Enable timely decisions and resource commitment
- Improve accountability for value delivered to the organization

### Typical Communication
- Steering reviews, milestone checkpoints, and executive updates
- Decision requests and approval communications
- Business outcome reviews and stakeholder briefings

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to confirm priorities and success criteria
- Provides direction to Project Managers on urgency, trade-offs, and escalation paths
- Receives updates from QA and delivery leads regarding readiness and outcomes

---

## DevOps / Release Manager

### Role Summary
DevOps or Release Managers ensure that software can be built, deployed, monitored, and recovered reliably. They coordinate operational readiness and deployment safety across the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Manage deployment pipelines, environments, and release coordination
- Support release planning, rollback readiness, and post-deploy verification
- Monitor platform health, service dependencies, and environment readiness
- Partner with Developers and Security Leads on deployment and operational safeguards
- Help document operational procedures and release communication plans

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk and release friction
- Improve service reliability and deployment repeatability
- Support fast, safe, and observable delivery to production

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment check-ins
- Incident and rollback coordination with delivery and support teams
- Status updates to PMs and stakeholders around release progress

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to validate deployment readiness and environment stability
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release windows and cross-team dependencies
- Collaborates with Security Leads on secure deployment practices and operational controls

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads protect the project from avoidable security and compliance risk. They help the team design, implement, and validate secure practices across the product lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Evaluate security requirements, risks, and compliance considerations
- Review designs, integrations, and release plans for security implications
- Support threat modeling, secure coding practices, and remediation planning
- Partner with DevOps and engineering teams to enforce secure deployment controls
- Coordinate security escalation paths when incidents or vulnerabilities are discovered

### Goals
- Minimize security exposure and operational risk
- Improve secure-by-default delivery practices
- Balance business velocity with security and compliance requirements

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and design feedback loops
- Risk and incident escalation updates with leadership and delivery teams
- Compliance and release gating discussions with PMs and stakeholders

### Interaction with existing roles
- Advises Developers on secure implementation patterns and remediation priorities
- Works with Product Managers and Sponsors on risk acceptance and business trade-offs
- Coordinates with Project Managers and Release Managers to ensure secure release gates are met

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles are intended to clarify accountability, reduce overlap, and improve project outcomes by making responsibilities explicit across cross-functional teams.
